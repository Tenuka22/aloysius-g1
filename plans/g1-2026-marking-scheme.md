## 6.5 Transfer Applications (Maximum Marks 100)

### Eligibility

1. Applicable only to permanent employees of the Government / Semi-Government / Statutory Boards / State Banks.
2. Having received a transfer within a period of less than 5 years as of June 30, 2026.
3. Residing in the feeder area belonging to the school after the transfer (at a distance of approximately 10 km).
4. Only the most recently received transfer should be considered. (Attachments, training courses, and study pursuits will not be considered.)
5. The distance from the previous place of service to the new place of service must be at least 50 km.
6. Documents required to prove residence must be considered.

### 6.5.1 Distance from the previous place of service to the new place of service (Maximum Marks 35)

| Distance | Marks |
|---|---|
| Over 150 km | 35 |
| 150 – 100 km | 28 |
| 100 – 50 km | 21 |

> The distance between the 2 service stations must be at least 50 km and service must have been rendered for a minimum of 1 year. Public transport route.
